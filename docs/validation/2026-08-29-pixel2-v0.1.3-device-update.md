## Runtime smoke

The device media validator launched `N64/SUPERMARIO64.Z64` through
`standalone:mupen64plus` for ten seconds:

```text
startup=pass
screen=pass
audio=pass
battery=62%
```

The captured 640x480 logical frame was upright and retained the expected 4:3
layout. ALSA playback advanced. The emulator exited, left no launch-private
cache or emulator process, and restored one frontend process. The packaged
`RiceVideoLinux.ini` retained SHA-256
`ec89fe5ab5760b94b822b41dc2889afc280a138fe1cb43811e1346b539850be9`.
A final DRM readback showed the normal six-system frontend grid, Wi-Fi status,
and battery status.

After healthy promotion and evidence capture, the device-specific transition
archive was removed from the update inbox. Its local evidence copy remains
under `output/live/2026-08-29-pixel2-v0.1.3-device-update/`. The public signed
v0.1.2-to-v0.1.3 update remains the release asset.

## Acceptance boundary

This validates the exact formal v0.1.3 Runtime payload, transactional update,
full managed-file readback, mutable-data preservation, frontend and Wi-Fi
restoration, and standalone N64 startup/display/audio on real Pixel2 hardware.
Physical Function switching, controls, exit, and SRAM load were already
operator-accepted on the immediately preceding functional Runtime. A fresh SD
image was not written in this update-only pass.
